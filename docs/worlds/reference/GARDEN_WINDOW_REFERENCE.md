## 1. Place identity

These anchors must remain recognisable in every Scene and every Daypart:

1. A wide window with a garden visible through it, giving outdoor depth.
2. A wooden table beneath the window.
3. A terracotta potted plant on the sill — the World's focal point.
4. The garden beyond: low beds, a hedge, one small tree. It must never resemble Quiet Trail's tree on a hill.
5. The edge of a home interior: a wall and the window frame.

## 2. Scenes

### `garden_window.tend` — role `tend`

**Activities:** tidyOneSurface, activeHouseholdTask, unhurriedTidyPause.

**Emotional job:** a small, cared-for order.

**Hero composition:** a cleared table with a folded cloth and a small wicker basket holding a few things; the plant on the sill; the garden behind.

**Card companion composition:** the basket and the folded cloth on the edge of the table, beside the pot.

**Mandatory anchors:** the table, the window, the sill plant.

**Optional objects:** the basket, the cloth, a watering can.

**Never:** mess or any "before / after" framing; cleaning products or brands; rubber gloves; checklists; appliances or screens; laundry piles.

### `garden_window.comfort` — role `comfort`

**Activities:** smallComfortRitual.

**Emotional job:** a warm pause with nothing to finish.

**Hero composition:** a single steaming cup on the sill or the table by the window, the plant, and the garden beyond.

**Card companion composition:** the cup with a wisp of steam, beside the pot.

**Mandatory anchors:** the cup, the window, the plant.

**Optional objects:** a kettle, a small plate, a folded napkin.

**Never:** a second cup or any paired place setting; food spreads or packaging; a phone; anything framed as a reward.

## 3. Daypart direction

- **Morning (05:00–11:59):** fresh, cool light through the window; the garden dewy; steam visible.
- **Day (12:00–17:59):** full, calm daylight; the garden bright but muted.
- **Evening (18:00–04:59):** a single warm pendant or table lamp over the table is the dominant light. The garden becomes a soft dark silhouette, the glass dark blue; a faint moon may appear, provided the lamp remains clearly dominant. No sunset. A warm drink at 02:00 is credible; the scene must remain credible at 18:00, 02:00 and 04:59.

## 4. Dark-theme notes

At evening the garden becomes very dark, so the lit table and the window frame carry the image. There are no reflections in the glass: they could show a figure and they muddy the dark card surface.

## 5. Differentiation

- **From Quiet Trail:** seen from inside, through a frame; a small cultivated garden instead of open landscape; no path; the garden's tree is small and near, never on a hill.
- **From Still Lake:** framed and domestic; no water.
- **From Open Room:** furnished, with a table and objects; the window shows a garden, whereas Open Room's windows show only sky.
- **From Reading Nook:** an outward-looking table and window instead of an inward-looking armchair and lamp; no bookshelf, no armchair.

## 6. Hero ↔ Card continuity

Shared: the table or sill, the plant, the Scene object, the direction of the window light (or lamp light in the evening), the palette and the Daypart. The Card may reduce the garden's depth to a Mist Sage wash in the window.
