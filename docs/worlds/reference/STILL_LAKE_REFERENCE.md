## 1. Place identity

These anchors must remain recognisable in every Scene and every Daypart:

1. A level, mirror-calm water surface with a soft reflection.
2. A low, flat far shoreline — a soft tree line, never rolling hills.
3. One flat shore rock — the World's focal point.
4. One reed cluster, on one side only.
5. A wide sky occupying at least half of the frame.

## 2. Scenes

### `still_lake.breathe` — role `breathe`

**Activities:** energisingBreathReset, focusedBreathingCount, restfulBreathingPause, quietSittingOutside.

**Emotional job:** give the breath somewhere to go — an open, level, unhurried space that also says "you can sit here".

**Hero composition:** seen from the shore. Water fills the lower third to half of the frame. The horizon is very low and perfectly level — the only level horizon among the V1 Worlds. The rock sits off-centre right in the near middle ground, the reeds at the left edge, the far shore faintly reflected. Sky occupies roughly 55% of the frame.

**Card companion composition:** a horizontal vignette of the rock, a band of water and a tuft of reeds, feathered at the top and left edges.

**Mandatory anchors:** the water surface, the rock, the level horizon.

**Optional objects:** the reeds, one distant water bird, a band of mist, a smooth pebble on the rock.

**Never:** boats, swimmers, fishing gear; a meditation cushion or mat (it turns the scene into "practice"); waves or wind; a sunset reflection path; towels; ripples that suggest something was thrown.

## 3. Daypart direction

- **Morning (05:00–11:59):** mist lying on the water, a pale Cream sky, a soft and blurred reflection; cool.
- **Day (12:00–17:59):** mist cleared, a clear pale sky, a crisp but soft reflection of the far shore; no glittering sparkle.
- **Evening (18:00–04:59):** sky and water in nearly the same deep blue-grey. Either a muted moon with a faint reflection column, or no moon and two or three restrained stars; the moon is the dominant light. The rock carries a faint cool rim light. No sunset. Credible at 18:00, 02:00 and 04:59.

## 4. Dark-theme notes

Evening water and sky have very little contrast and risk disappearing on the dark card surface. The rock stays the lightest mid-tone mass in every Card artwork, and the moon's reflection provides the highlight.

## 5. Differentiation

- **From Quiet Trail:** level and horizontal instead of winding and rising; no path; no tree as focus; water dominates.
- **From Open Room:** outdoors; no architecture; light comes from sky and water, not from a window.
- **From Reading Nook:** open and exposed instead of enclosed; no interior.
- **From Garden Window:** seen directly rather than through a frame; wild water instead of a cultivated garden.

## 6. Hero ↔ Card continuity

Shared: the rock, the water, the level horizon, the light direction, the palette and the Daypart. The Card may omit the far-shore detail, the reflection and the reeds.
