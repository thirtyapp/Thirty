## A. Strategic thesis

**THIRTY is the flagship and master product.** It is not merely the first of several unrelated apps that happen to share a publisher.

The long-term opportunity is a **recognizable THIRTY product family**, built around the same core philosophy, visual language and Circle concept. Future specialist products may exist under the THIRTY family. The initial candidates are:

- **THIRTY Fit**
- **THIRTY Meditation**
- **THIRTY Yoga**

These are **working strategic names only**. They are not approved store names, trademarks, launch commitments or current implementation scope. None of them is a current commitment.

The purpose is **not simply "more apps"**. A portfolio of loosely related apps would split attention, multiply maintenance and dilute the brand. The opportunity is a **coherent ecosystem** in which the following may reinforce each other over time:

- brand recognition of THIRTY itself;
- the Circle as a shared behavioral mechanic;
- the World system and its illustration language;
- Plans as a recurring-value mechanism;
- personalization that stays local, honest and useful;
- software foundations proven in the flagship;
- later, physical ritual products that belong to a THIRTY ritual.

The sequencing rule governs everything below:

> **"First prove THIRTY. Then expand the system."**

This strategy must not delay the flagship, change V1, or create a platform-rewrite project.

## C. Brand architecture

**THIRTY remains the broad flagship health and wellbeing product.** Its job is the one already defined in [VISION.md](../VISION.md) and the Playbook: the best investment of about thirty minutes, today.

**Do not hollow out THIRTY to make room for specialist apps.** A family strategy that deliberately weakens the flagship in order to "save" depth for a sibling app would break the flagship's promise and its Free integrity. The flagship may itself deepen later, when separately justified, with:

- more activities;
- more Plans;
- a richer Coach;
- richer Insights;
- richer Worlds and Atmosphere.

### Preferred working family pattern

```
THIRTY
THIRTY Fit
THIRTY Meditation
THIRTY Yoga
```

- The **THIRTY wordmark remains the master identity.** The descriptor (Fit, Meditation, Yoga) is secondary in hierarchy and visual weight.
- **The original product remains named THIRTY** unless that is explicitly changed later. It does not become "THIRTY Core", "THIRTY Daily" or similar by default.
- **Final product names still require** a later trademark, store-availability and positioning review.

### Shared family cues

Family members may share:

- the Circle;
- the wordmark;
- typography;
- the sage/cream identity;
- a related dark-mode philosophy;
- watercolor Worlds;
- daypart awareness;
- calm interaction design;
- a non-judgmental tone;
- one clear primary action per screen.

A specialist app **may** develop a distinct secondary identity and its own specialist Worlds. It must **never** become an ordinary category app with a THIRTY logo pasted onto it. If removing the logo would leave a generic fitness, meditation or yoga app, the product has failed the family test.

## E. Non-negotiable THIRTY DNA

Every family product must preserve the following. These are constraints, not aspirations.

1. **The Circle has behavioral meaning.** It is the daily unit of attention and the shape progress takes ([Playbook Ch.1 §3](../playbook/01-the-circle-manifesto.md#3-the-meaning-of-the-circle)) — not just a progress ring or a logo.
2. **No guilt mechanics.** No streak pressure, missed-day debt, leaderboards, social comparison, punitive resets or "failure" language ([Playbook Ch.1 §7–§8](../playbook/01-the-circle-manifesto.md#7-why-completion-matters-more-than-streaks)).
3. **One manageable next step.** The product reduces decision fatigue; it does not create endless browsing or a content shop.
4. **Premium deepens rather than gates.** Core product value must feel whole ([ADR-007](../product/adr/ADR-007-premium-never-blocks-the-core-loop.md), [PREMIUM_STRATEGY.md](../product/PREMIUM_STRATEGY.md)). Each specialist's Free/Premium line requires its own explicit decision, under the same principle.
5. **Restrained health claims.** Category expansion does not authorize unsupported medical, therapeutic, rehabilitation or physiological claims.
6. **Calm is a product property.** Even active products must remain recognizably THIRTY: calmer, simpler and more human than the alternative ([Brand Book §1](../BRAND_BOOK.md#1-the-golden-rule)).

Explicit examples:

- **THIRTY Fit must not become grind culture.**
- **THIRTY Yoga must not become performance comparison.**
- **THIRTY Meditation must not present itself as therapy.**

## F. THIRTY Fit — candidate

**Candidate job:** a deeper movement and fitness path for people who want more structure than the broad flagship should provide, while retaining THIRTY's calm and approachable character.

**Candidate direction:**

- richer movement and fitness Plans;
- structured exercise Circles;
- exercise guidance visible within the Circle where useful;
- authored progression;
- beginner-friendly alternatives;
- lighter variants on any given day;
- specialist Worlds that remain recognizably THIRTY.

**Rejected by default:**

- calorie targets;
- body comparison;
- public ranking;
- personal-record pressure;
- "no excuses" language and other grind-culture framing ([Brand Book §22](../BRAND_BOOK.md#22-social-media-style));
- unsupported physiological prescription (heart-rate zones, intensity targets, corrective-exercise claims) unless a later, explicit safety and expertise decision exists.

**Potential territory:** strength, mobility, functional movement, gentle progression, sustainable physical capability, and body confidence without comparison.

**Brand principle:**

> **"THIRTY Fit may deepen physical capability without turning health into competition."**

This makes THIRTY Fit compatible with the existing THIRTY Brand Promise rather than an exception to it. See [K2](#k2-world-versus-instructional-guidance) for the full calm-fitness constraint and for how exercise guidance may show a human demonstrator.

**Gate:** THIRTY Fit only earns a standalone app if the required depth **cannot** be served well as a bounded set of Plans and activities inside flagship THIRTY.

## G. THIRTY Meditation — candidate

**Candidate job:** a deeper meditation, breathing and attention practice for users who want it as a repeatable path rather than an occasional activity inside THIRTY.

**Candidate direction:**

- meditation Plans;
- routines;
- guided Circle Sessions (in the Playbook's sense of a single guided or paced Circle experience, [Ch.2 §9](../playbook/02-circle-experience-system.md#9-premium-experience));
- breathing and attention practices within explicit safety boundaries;
- daypart-aware practices;
- calm audio or atmosphere, if separately approved;
- gentle resumption after gaps.

**Explicit boundary:** THIRTY Meditation is **not therapy, not diagnosis and not a replacement for professional mental-health care**, and must never present itself as such.

**Gate:** a standalone app only if the domain proves enough depth, repeated use and commercial value beyond simply adding more meditation content to THIRTY.

## H. THIRTY Yoga — candidate

**Candidate job:** a deeper yoga, mobility and stretching experience with THIRTY's approachable, non-performance-oriented philosophy.

**Candidate direction:**

- yoga Plans;
- mobility and stretching Plans;
- pose or movement guidance in the Circle where useful;
- short routines;
- longer authored paths;
- lighter alternatives;
- calm visual instruction;
- specialist but recognizably THIRTY Worlds.

**Boundary:** no unsupported medical, rehabilitation or injury-treatment claims; no prescribed ranges of motion presented as correction.

**Visual guidance:** where a pose or movement must be shown, it is shown through the separate instructional guidance layer defined in [K2](#k2-world-versus-instructional-guidance), never by placing a figure in the World.

**Gate:** standalone status still requires a distinct job and sufficient depth.

## I. Flagship breadth versus specialist depth

> **THIRTY provides breadth across daily wellbeing.
> Specialist THIRTY apps provide depth inside one domain.**

This is the central product distinction of the family.

**The portfolio idea must not freeze flagship THIRTY** at three Plans or at an artificially small catalogue forever. The flagship's Plan and activity catalogue may expand beyond the initial inventory after evidence and quality review. This document deliberately chooses **no** permanent catalogue count.

### Standalone-product rule

If the entire specialist proposition can be delivered cleanly through a few additional flagship activities or Plans, **do not make another app.**

A separate app becomes more justified when the domain needs:

- substantial Plan depth;
- different instructional needs;
- its own repeated user intent;
- additional safety rules;
- deeper content systems;
- a distinct recurring commercial proposition.

## J. Plans across the family

Plans are likely to remain a major recurring-value mechanism across the THIRTY family, consistent with Plans being the first Premium pillar in [PREMIUM_STRATEGY.md §3](../product/PREMIUM_STRATEGY.md#3-premium-priority-order).

The current V1 Plan inventory is **not a permanent ceiling**. However:

- never inflate Plan counts for catalogue optics;
- every Plan needs a distinct user need and a meaningful sequence;
- Plans should remain reusable across cycles;
- no monthly content treadmill, and no new content tied to renewal;
- specialist products may eventually carry richer Plan catalogues;
- Plan structures may differ between products where the user's job genuinely requires it;
- a bundle can never be justified simply by multiplying shallow Plans.

The current V1 Plan contract itself is governed by the recurring Premium freeze and is not changed here.

## K. Worlds

Worlds are a major candidate for family recognition — arguably the most distinctive one after the Circle.

Future products may reuse:

- the World concept ("a place, not an illustration", [WORLD_SYSTEM.md](../worlds/WORLD_SYSTEM.md));
- the Hero/Card relationship;
- daypart logic;
- the seasonal philosophy;
- the illustration language ([ILLUSTRATION_LANGUAGE.md](../illustration/ILLUSTRATION_LANGUAGE.md));
- selected existing Worlds, **only** where semantically correct.

Specialist Worlds may be created later. **Do not force an existing World into another product merely to save production work.**

> **Different places inside the same THIRTY universe,
> not reskinned copies of one app.**

All World rules — including [§2 No People](../worlds/WORLD_SYSTEM.md#2-no-people) — continue to apply to any family product unless explicitly amended through World System governance.

## K2. World versus instructional guidance

*Founder decision, 7 October 2026. Resolves the earlier recorded tension between exercise/pose guidance and World System §2.*

### The architectural distinction

> **WORLD = atmosphere, place, emotional identity.**
> **GUIDANCE LAYER = practical visual instruction when body position matters.**

- **World artwork remains people-free.** [WORLD_SYSTEM.md §2](../worlds/WORLD_SYSTEM.md#2-no-people) stays fully in force for every World, Hero and Card, in the flagship and in every family product. No identifiable people in World Hero art, no fitness bodies as World focal subjects, no avatars as part of the World, no human silhouettes as the primary World subject. This applies unchanged to all current V1 World artwork.
- **Instructional human guidance is allowed** where an activity cannot be communicated well without demonstrating body position or movement — for example yoga, mobility, stretching where pose or form matters, strength or exercise guidance, and future THIRTY Fit activity guidance.
- Such a figure appears only in a **separate guidance layer or activity demonstration**, which may sit within a Circle Session or specialist product experience. It is not part of the World artwork, and the underlying World never becomes a people-led illustration.
- Guidance-layer figures must not become the World's emotional focal point, must avoid idealised, competitive or physique-led imagery, and may be used only under an approved activity or product contract (World System §2, scope clarification v1.2.0).

This is an explicit clarification of scope, not a repeal of the No People rule.

### Optional guide preference

Future products may optionally ask which visual guide the user prefers:

> **"Which guide would you prefer to see?"** — Man · Woman · No preference

This selects the instructional guide only, to improve comfort and identification during movement instruction. It is **not** a question about the user's own sex or gender identity, and must never be framed as one.

**Privacy and product boundaries:**

- optional; no answer is required;
- "No preference" is always available;
- no effect on access, recommendations, pricing or health interpretation;
- sex or gender is never inferred from any other data;
- never used for analytics targeting;
- stored locally by default, unless a later, explicit cross-device identity architecture is approved (§L);
- the user can always change or remove it.

### THIRTY Fit calm-fitness constraint

THIRTY Fit may deepen strength, mobility, functional movement, gentle progression, sustainable physical capability and body confidence without comparison. It continues to reject leaderboards, physique comparison, calorie obsession, "no excuses" language, punishment for missed days, aggressive transformation promises, streak pressure and grind culture.

> **"THIRTY Fit may deepen physical capability without turning health into competition."**

### Scope

No implementation is approved by this section. It does not add yoga or Fit to V1, change the current World assets, create onboarding work, approve collection of gender identity, add cloud profiles, or change Free/Premium, monetization or release timing.

## L. Personalization and cross-app identity

**Current local-first personalization remains valid.** Nothing here changes it. A future optional guide preference ([K2](#k2-world-versus-instructional-guidance)) follows the same local-first rule.

A product family **may** later create a need for:

- shared identity;
- preference portability;
- subscription recognition across apps;
- cross-app continuity.

**That does not authorize accounts or cloud infrastructure now.** The current boundaries — no mandatory account, no general backend, store-based entitlement identity kept separate from local data — remain in force.

Before any shared-identity work is even proposed, a future decision must define:

- the exact user problem being solved;
- the exact data that must cross apps;
- what remains local;
- consent;
- deletion;
- whether each app still works fully independently;
- which subscription or store requirement actually needs the system.

> **Do not build account infrastructure merely because multiple THIRTY apps may exist someday.**

## M. Commercial architecture

The following are recorded as **future candidates only**, not approved packaging:

- separate Premium per app;
- a cross-product THIRTY membership or pass;
- a multi-product bundle;
- app-specific Premium plus an ecosystem upgrade;
- physical ritual products;
- relevant future partnerships or programs.

Rules:

- **No current price, trial or promotion automatically transfers** to future specialist apps. The flagship's current monthly price hypothesis remains governed solely by the recurring Premium freeze.
- **No bundle may be promised** before app-store, billing and RevenueCat feasibility have been verified.

### Cross-selling

Cross-selling can be useful — for example, a movement-oriented THIRTY user may later discover THIRTY Fit. Cross-sell must be:

- sparse;
- calm;
- relevant to the user;
- only for an actual, available product.

**The flagship must not become an advertising surface for other KEYBRACE products.** Existing rules on invitation timing, haptics and no interruptive sales prompts apply to cross-sell exactly as they apply to Premium.

## O. Technical reuse

**The future family is not permission for a platform rewrite.**

> **"Build THIRTY for THIRTY.
> Keep its boundaries clean enough that proven reusable pieces can later be extracted."**

Potential future reuse candidates **may** include:

- Circle lifecycle and state primitives;
- daypart utilities;
- brand and design tokens;
- World rendering contracts;
- reminders;
- local preference patterns;
- Plan primitives;
- entitlement abstractions;
- accessibility patterns;
- analytics and privacy infrastructure;
- release and QA infrastructure.

**This is architectural foresight, not an extraction backlog.**

Do **not**:

- create a shared SDK before a real second consumer exists;
- refactor stable flagship code solely for hypothetical reuse;
- create multi-app configuration layers now;
- generalize content schemas for imagined categories;
- delay the flagship launch to become "portfolio ready".

> **"Reuse concepts first.
> Extract shared code only when a concrete second product proves the reuse."**

This is consistent with the existing engineering rule against over-engineering in the project's development handbook (`CLAUDE.md`).

## P. Standalone product gate

A specialist app should only progress if **all ten** questions can be answered positively:

1. Does it solve a recurring and sufficiently distinct job?
2. Is there enough depth to justify a separate product?
3. Can it stay recognizably THIRTY?
4. Is there credible user evidence?
5. Is there a plausible commercial case?
6. Is there a believable distribution and cross-sell path?
7. Can KEYBRACE maintain it without damaging THIRTY?
8. Can it remain useful without a content treadmill?
9. Can useful foundations be reused without a platform rewrite?
10. Will users understand why it exists separately from THIRTY?

**Default when these are not satisfied: deepen THIRTY rather than create another app.**

## Q. Sequencing

### Phase A — Prove the flagship

Current priority:

- finish THIRTY pre-release;
- live billing proof;
- launch;
- real product usage;
- learn which intentions, activities and Plans create repeated value;
- validate audience and willingness to pay.

**No specialist implementation in this phase.**

### Phase B — Deepen THIRTY

Use evidence to consider:

- more activities;
- more Plans;
- a richer Coach;
- richer Insights;
- Atmosphere;
- ritual products.

Do not jump to a new app when flagship expansion solves the need.

### Phase C — Select one specialist

If evidence supports a vertical, build **one** specialist first. Do not begin Fit, Meditation and Yoga simultaneously.

Phase C requires a dedicated, founder-approved product brief covering:

- user;
- problem / job;
- catalogue;
- Free/Premium boundary;
- Plans;
- Worlds;
- safety;
- reuse;
- acquisition;
- economics.

### Phase D — Prove the family model

Only after the first specialist proves the operational and commercial family thesis, consider:

- another specialist;
- ecosystem membership;
- shared account or sync;
- a larger merchandise system;
- broader ecosystem marketing.

## R. Social and distribution implication

While THIRTY is the only actual product, **the master THIRTY brand carries the early audience.**

- Do not split marketing resources across multiple active specialist brands before those products exist.
- Handles and names can be reserved defensively.
- When a specialist product becomes real, decide separately between a launch from the master account, a dedicated specialist account, or a combined model.
- Brand tone ([Brand Book §20–§22](../BRAND_BOOK.md#22-social-media-style)) and health-claim restraint remain shared across every account.
- Do not market specialist products, or capabilities they would have, before they exist.
