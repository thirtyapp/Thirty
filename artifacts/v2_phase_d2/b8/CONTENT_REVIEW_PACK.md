- Ending: Slow down for the last few minutes on the way back.

- Needs: moreEnergy (primary), clearerHead (secondary) · 5–10 min · open

- Preparation: Something that plays music.
- While you’re there: Start small — sway, tap, walk around the room.
- While you’re there: Let the next song be a bit livelier.
- While you’re there: Nobody’s watching. There’s no right way to do this.

- Ending: Let the last song be a slower one, then sit for a moment.

- While you’re there: Notice three things you haven’t noticed before.

#### A quick standing stretch

- Needs: moreEnergy (primary), clearerHead (secondary) · 5–5 min · guidedSteps
- Why (moreEnergy): A short standing sequence for when you’ve been still.
- First action: Stand up and reach both arms overhead.
- Preparation: Enough space to stretch your arms out.
- Step — Reach up: Reach both arms overhead and stretch tall, then lower.
- Step — Side bend: One arm overhead, lean gently to the other side. Then switch.
- Step — Shoulder rolls: Roll your shoulders back in slow circles, then forward.
- Step — Gentle twist: Hands on hips, turn your upper body slowly left, then right.
- Step — March: March on the spot, lifting your knees a little.
- Lighter: Just the reaches and the shoulder rolls.
- Safety: Move only as far as is comfortable. Skip anything that hurts.
- Ending: Finish with one more long reach, then let your arms drop.

- Needs: moreEnergy (primary), clearerHead (secondary) · 10–15 min · open

- While you’re there: Put some music on if it helps.
- While you’re there: Stop when your time is up, even if the job isn’t finished.

#### One task, full attention

- Needs: clearerHead (primary) · 20–25 min · open

#### Gentle stretch pause

- Needs: moreEnergy (secondary), clearerHead (secondary), gentlerPace (primary) · 8–10 min · guidedSteps
- Why (gentlerPace): Slow, easy stretches that don’t ask much of you.
- First action: Sit on the edge of a chair, or stand somewhere with a little space.
- Step — Neck: Let your head tip slowly towards one shoulder, then the other.
- Step — Shoulders: Lift your shoulders up towards your ears, then let them drop.
- Step — Open the chest: Hold the sides of the chair, or clasp your hands behind you, and gently open your chest.
- Step — Seated twist: Sit tall and turn gently to one side, one hand on the chair. Then the other side.
- Step — Fold forward: Let your upper body fold forward slowly, arms heavy. Come up slowly.
- Lighter: Just the neck and shoulders.
- Safety: Stay well within what feels comfortable. Skip anything that hurts.
- Ending: Sit still for a moment before you get up.

- Needs: clearerHead (secondary), gentlerPace (primary) · 10–15 min · open

- While you’re there: Let your eyes rest on something far away.
- While you’re there: Listen to what you can hear, near and far.

- While you’re there: Hold the cup in both hands for a moment.
- While you’re there: Keep your phone out of reach while you drink.

#### Tend to one small thing

- Needs: clearerHead (secondary), gentlerPace (primary) · 10–15 min · open
