## A. Today

**The promise:**
- Free: "Here’s one thing for today."
- Premium: "Here’s your Circle for today."
- Basis lines:
  - "Small parts, one after another, to fit your 20 minutes." (first use)
  - "Built from things you’ve found useful, to fit your 20 minutes." (only with explicit answers)
  - "Today, one activity fits better than a mix." (honest fallback)

**Need and time:**
- More Energy: "I want to feel a little more awake and active."
- Clearer Head: "I want fewer things competing for my attention, and one thing to focus on."
- Gentler Pace: "I want something gentle that doesn't feel like another demand."
- Times: About 10 minutes · About 20 minutes · Up to 30 minutes.

**Representative activities** (Free's one activity; 16 live in the catalogue):

| Activity | Why line | First action |
|---|---|---|
| **Write it down** (Clearer Head) | "Get it onto paper so you don’t have to hold it all." | "Grab paper or a blank note and write the first thing on your mind." |
| **Phone-free walk** | "Nothing to check, so your attention can follow one thing." | "Put your phone on silent, out of sight in a pocket or bag, and start walking." |
| **Move to music** (More Energy) | "A few songs you like, and moving however feels good." | "Put on a song you really like and turn it up a little." |

**Feedback:**
- Did you try it? Yes · A little · Not today
- Was it useful? Very useful · Somewhat useful · Not useful
- Both are optional.

**Exclusions:**
- **Not this one today:** "Can’t go outside" · "Too much for today" · "Not feeling this one". One a day.
- **Skip this part?** "Skip it for today" ("The next part begins. Nothing is recorded against this one.") · "Leave it out next time" (Premium only — "THIRTY won’t put it in your Circles again. You can bring it back in What THIRTY remembers.") · "Keep going".
- **Don’t suggest** and **Put it back**: in What THIRTY remembers.

**Kept Circles:** "Keep this Circle to use again on a day you choose." → "Kept as My gentle Circle. It’s in Yours, under You." · "A Circle you kept." · "Use it today".

## E. Premium offer (founder-approved, unchanged since B7)

- **Headline:** "A Circle made for your day"
- **Promise:** "One guided Circle for today, made from the time you have — and, over the weeks, a direction for your Circles and what they show."
- **Made for you:** tell THIRTY what would help most and how much time you have, and it puts together a new Circle for today, sized to that time
- **Guided for you:** THIRTY’s own voice tells you when each part begins, so you needn’t keep watching the screen *(in a build without the voice: "one part at a time, moving on when each part’s time is up — nothing to plan or keep track of")*
- **Plans to follow:** each Plan is five Circles with a direction, on the days you choose its need
- **Insights:** when your own Circles and answers show something, THIRTY says what it noticed, and may suggest one change for you to make or leave
- **Shaped by your answers:** tell THIRTY what was useful and later Circles can bring it back; what you leave out stays out
- **Free:** "With Free, THIRTY still gives you one good thing for today, and learns from what you tell it. What it remembers, your History, the Circles you keep and reminders stay free."
- **Price:** the store's localized monthly price, then "Billed monthly. Renews automatically until you cancel." · "Become Premium" · "Not now" · "What you keep stays yours, with or without Premium."

## G. Remaining founder flags

1. **More Energy is thin**: 6 main pieces and 19 materially distinct sessions, at ≈10 and at up to 30. Over a year at ≈10, the first exact repeat came on day 23, with no stall.
   - **Classification:** WEAK.
   - **Blocker status:** not a D2 blocker. The public-release safety review is still required, and the gated exertion and breathing content stays off.
   - **Real-user risk:** yes.
2. **Legacy "Your routine"**: unreachable on a clean install.
   - It appears only for History records written by the retired Phase D routines.
   - Production never writes such records again; the only reader is a migration key marked "No longer written", and there is no import.
   - **Disposition:** legacy development-data compatibility only. No fix.
3. **Apostrophes**: 9 live strings use ' while 69 use ’.
   - Among them is the founder-approved "Begin today's Circle".
   - No Brand Book or Design System rule covers this.
   - **Disposition:** non-blocking editorial inconsistency. Not changed.
4. **New in B9: one calendar label is garbled by the S25's Samsung speech engine.**
   - The phone's speech engine garbles "Tuesday, October 20, 2026, Circle recorded, not closed".
   - Every other label tested is clean: Tuesday 6, Saturday 3 with the same "recorded, not closed" wording, and 18.
   - THIRTY sends one correct, complete utterance; the captions confirm it, with no re-announcement and no app audio.
   - The same label is clean on Google's speech engine on the same phone.
   - A day-first date format did not help, so it was reverted byte-for-byte.
   - **Disposition:** a device speech-engine defect, not an app defect. Non-blocking; recorded for the audits.
5. *(Carried, not new)*: older Free activity wording ("turn it up a little", "a bit livelier") stays on the release-audit list (ADR-023, `final_audit/RELEASE_AUDIT_NOTES.md`). It isn't spoken, and it is not an efficacy claim.
